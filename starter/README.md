## Requirements

- **Write.as Pro.**
- **Your blog on its own address** (`yourblog.writeas.com` or a custom domain), if the template uses JavaScript.

## Install

1. Open **Write.as → Blogs → your blog → Customize**.
2. Paste `theme.css` into **Custom CSS** and `theme.js` into **Custom JavaScript**.
3. Edit the settings at the top of each file, then **Save changes**.

## Settings

| Setting | What to put there |
|---|---|
| `BLOG_ALIAS` | Your blog's short name, the part after `write.as/` in its address. |
